Loan Settlement

Struggling With EMIs? Get Loan Settlement Help

If multiple EMIs or mounting debt is stressing you out, our settlement experts can help you negotiate a reduced one-time payoff with your lender.

What Is Loan Settlement?

Loan settlement is a negotiation process where a borrower repays a lump sum amount that is lower than the total outstanding dues, and the lender agrees to close the account. It is usually considered when a borrower is unable to continue regular EMI payments due to job loss, medical emergency, or a business downturn.

Our team analyses your loan portfolio, communicates with your bank or NBFC on your behalf, and works towards a settlement that is realistic for you and acceptable to the lender.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying for a loan with CreditBee Finance.

How quickly will I receive the loan amount?
Once your application is submitted and documents are verified, the loan is typically approved within a few minutes. After approval and e-sign, the amount is credited directly to your bank account — in most cases within 10 minutes. Actual disbursal time may vary slightly depending on your bank's processing speed.
What is the maximum and minimum loan amount I can get?
CreditBee Finance facilitates personal loans ranging from ₹6,000 up to ₹10,00,000. The final approved amount depends on your monthly income, existing obligations, credit score, employment type and the lender's internal policy. Higher amounts may be available for secured loan categories like Loan Against Property.
What are the interest rate and processing fee?
Interest rates start from just 5% and go up to 27.5% per annum depending on your credit profile and the partner lender. The processing fee is up to 4.7% + GST of the sanctioned amount, or a flat ₹1,250 depending on the loan size and lender. All charges are fully disclosed in your sanction letter before you accept the offer.
What documents do I need to submit?
For most loan products you need: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, a recent bank statement (3–6 months), and proof of income (salary slips or ITR for self-employed applicants). All documents can be uploaded digitally — no physical submission is required.
Do I need to provide collateral or security?
No. Personal, business, education and wedding loans offered through CreditBee Finance are unsecured — you do not need to pledge any asset. Only secured products like Gold Loan and Loan Against Property require collateral.
Will my credit score be affected if I apply?
We first perform a soft eligibility check which does not impact your credit score. A hard enquiry is made only after you confirm your intent to proceed with a specific lender's offer.
Is CreditBee Finance available all over India?
Yes. CreditBee Finance is a 100% online platform serving customers across all states and union territories of India. You can apply from anywhere — metro cities, tier-2 towns or rural areas — as long as you have a valid mobile number, bank account and internet connection.
What is loan settlement and should I opt for it?
Loan settlement is a negotiated closure of a loan where the lender accepts a reduced lump-sum payment. It is generally recommended only when you are genuinely unable to continue paying EMIs. Note that a settled account is reported to credit bureaus and may impact your future creditworthiness. Our advisors will explain all pros and cons before you decide.
Are there any hidden charges?
No. We believe in complete transparency. All applicable charges — processing fee, interest rate, prepayment terms — are disclosed upfront in your sanction letter before you accept the offer. There are no surprise deductions.
